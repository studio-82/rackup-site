How it works

Rackup screens grants, tax credits and incentives from New York City, New York State, utilities and federal agencies, and shows which ones your business qualifies for.

  1. 01

    Profile your business

    Industry, location, size and the investments planned for the year ahead. It takes a few minutes.

  2. 02

    Review your matches

    Each program is checked against your profile, with the reasons it fits and anything left to confirm.

  3. 03

    Apply with full terms

    Award size, timing, required documents, and whether funds are paid in advance or reimbursed.

  4. 04

    Stay on top of new money

    Programs open, close and change all year. Rackup keeps checking and lets you know when a new one fits your business.
